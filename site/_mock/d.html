detent({ marks })

Marks pull. Strong marks hold.

Near a notch, the ball pulls the wheel in. The deep notches are strong marks, and they pull from further away.

detent(wheel, { loop: true, count: 16, marks })

detent({ coast })

Flick it. It glides, then lands.

Let go in the middle of a flick and it carries on, slows down, and stops on a mark. It never flies past and springs back.

detent(flywheel, { loop: true, count: 10, coast: 0.35 })

detentThrow()

Past the middle, it goes.

Let go past the middle and it snaps across. Short of it, it falls back, with no bounce.

detentThrow(lever, { onThrow: (way) => flip(way) })

detent3d()

Turn it, and it locks to a view.

Spin it sideways, and it lands on the nearest of eight views. The plunger drops into its slot.

detent3d(plate, { faces: 'turntable', views: 8 })

detentScroll()

This page clicks into place too.

On a computer, stop scrolling anywhere and the page settles on the nearest part. On a phone we leave your own scroll alone, so you can stop anywhere and use the controls. Any wheel or key stops a settle at once. With reduced motion there is no settle at all.
