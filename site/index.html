Controls that click into place.

Make your web app's sliders, pickers and sheets feel native. Marks pull, flicks glide, nothing bounces. Keyboard, screen readers and reduced motion are already done.

npm install @hlmnhkl/detent

Go on, give it a shove. It always lands on a number, and 0, 25, 50, 75 and 100 pull harder.

A price filter for a shop

Two thumbs, one track. RM 100, 250 and 500 pull harder, because that is where people stop.


          

          

        

        

          

            
A booking time picker

            
Quarter hours, with whole hours pulling harder. Flick it and it still stops on a slot.

            

          

          

        

        

          

            
An order sheet that rests

            
Peek, half or full, never in between. Drag the handle, flick it, or tap it.

            

          

          

        

        

          

            
A tip dial

            
One click per percent. 10, 15 and 20 pull harder, so the common tips are one easy turn.

            

          

          

        

      


      

        

          
Get started

          
Install it, then give it a value, the marks to land on, and the hand. detent moves the number. You draw it.

          

            npm install @hlmnhkl/detent
            
          

          

        

        
      


      

        
Why it is worth 4 KB

        
    
          
  • Native feelMarks pull, flicks glide, nothing bounces. Like the pickers on a phone.
    • 
          
  • Accessible by defaultArrow, Page, Home and End land where a drag would. One settled value for screen readers. Reduced motion lands at once.
    • 
          
  • About 4 KBGzipped, the whole engine.
    • 
          
  • No dependenciesPlain TypeScript, nothing else to install.
    • 
          
  • Any frameworkReact, Vue, Svelte or none. You keep your markup and your styles.
