Your checklist

  • Give it a name with aria-label or aria-labelledby.
  • Write good words with text: "RM 250" or "19:30", not "250".
  • Show focus with a :focus-visible style that passes 3:1 against what is around it.
  • Make the target big enough: at least 44 by 44 px for the part people grab.
  • Check touch-action. If your CSS sets none, detent sets pan-y on a horizontal control, pan-x on a vertical one and none on a turn or in 2D, so the page still scrolls past it.
  • Test with keys only, then with a screen reader, then with reduced motion on.
