detentHold()

Press and hold to confirm, on a fair clock. Let go early and it runs back. A tap, Enter or Space never confirms by itself: it asks your app for a confirm step.

Hold to confirm

Hold for 1.5 seconds, with a finger, the mouse, Enter or Space.

Ready.

.button::before { scale: var(--progress) 1; transform-origin: left; }

A fair clock

  • It fills at an even speed, from 0 to 1 over time (1500 ms). It is a clock, so it does not ease.
  • Let go early and it runs back in about 140 ms, easing out. onCancel fires.
  • A press under 250 ms, one Enter or Space, or a click with no press (a screen reader, voice control) calls onTap. It never confirms.
  • Holding Enter or Space works like a held finger. A finger that slides 16 px off the element, a page scroll that takes it, or focus moving away all cancel.
  • onConfirm fires once. It stays done, with data-done, until you call reset().
  • With reduced motion the clock still runs, because the time is the point. The rewind jumps.

Keys and screen readers

Holding a key is hard for many people, so it must never be the only way (WCAG 2.5.1 and 2.1.1). Use onTap to open a confirm step with Cancel and Confirm buttons, and move focus to Cancel. The hold to delete recipe does this.

Use a real <button type="button">, describe how it works with aria-describedby, and say the result in a polite live region. detentHold sets no role or ARIA of its own. It stops the long-press menu on the element, so a finger can hold it.

haptics: true ticks once when the hold completes. It works on Android, not on iPhone, because Safari has no vibration API. Use it for the clicks that matter, not every step.
