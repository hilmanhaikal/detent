detentScroll()

The page settles onto a section when the wheel, trackpad or finger stops. Strong sections pull harder, it never rests halfway between two close ones, and tall sections are free to read.

Try it on this page

This docs page does not settle on its own, because you are reading it. Turn it on, then scroll a little and let go.

Off. The page scrolls as usual.

Mark the sections in your HTML. data-mark="strong" pulls harder.

<section data-mark="strong" id="top">…</section>
<section data-mark id="pricing">…</section>

It never fights you

  • It moves only after the hand stops: about 140 ms after the last wheel step, or once a finger's momentum ends.
  • Any wheel, touch, key or mouse press during a settle stops it at once.
  • Keys, the scrollbar, find in page and screen readers scroll natively, with no settle after them.
  • A section taller than the screen is free in the middle. Only its edges pull.
  • In-page links glide to their section, then move focus to its heading, with no second jump.
  • With reduced motion there is no settle at all, and links jump.
